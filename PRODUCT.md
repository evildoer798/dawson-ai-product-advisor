# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are Dawson Vapor Technology product, market, and management teams evaluating disposable-vape concepts for overseas markets. The demo is also presented to customers and investors who need to understand why a recommendation was made, not merely see an AI-generated score.

## Product Purpose

Turn product images, specifications, and Dawson's market-intelligence snapshots into a bilingual, traceable opportunity assessment. Success means a user can move from upload to an explainable, exportable report in under three minutes and every material conclusion can be traced to evidence.

## Positioning

An evidence-led product opportunity assessment that keeps regulation, competition, supply chain feasibility, and commercial assumptions in one reproducible decision record.

## Brand Personality

Precise, composed, and globally informed. The interface should feel like a calm intelligence room: high-density information, clear hierarchy, and restrained confidence.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic chat assistant, a neon crypto dashboard, a playful consumer app, or a collection of interchangeable glass cards. Do not imply guaranteed sales success or expose model chain-of-thought.

## Design Principles

- Evidence before assertion: every important conclusion links to a source and date.
- Confidence is visible: missing, stale, or inconsistent data is surfaced rather than hidden.
- The workflow stays legible: show operational stages, never private model reasoning.
- Regulation is a gate, not decoration: hard compliance failures override opportunity signals.
- One decision record: inputs, scoring version, evidence, report, and expert feedback remain connected.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard operation. Support reduced motion, avoid color-only status communication, use bilingual labels consistently, and keep print output legible in grayscale.
